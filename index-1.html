How it works

Why Vaultform

The form tool the
internet was missing.

Every form you've ever filled lives on a server someone else controls. Vaultform changes that — permanently.

⬡
Tamper-proof records
Every submission gets a unique Walrus blob ID. If anyone changes the data, the ID changes. The original is always provable.
◈
Encrypted with Seal
Sensitive fields are encrypted before leaving your browser. Only the form creator's wallet can decrypt and read private responses.
✦
Permanent storage
Submissions survive startups shutting down, platforms changing policies, servers going offline. Walrus stores across hundreds of independent nodes.
◎
You own everything
No platform terms that can be revoked. No account to ban. Your Sui wallet is your identity. Your dashboard belongs to you alone.
